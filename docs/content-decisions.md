# Content decisions

Source: the "proposed information" document (9 Nov 2026). Status key: **Applied** = proposed default, now on the site; **Confirm** = on the site (usually next to a visible `[Confirm]` note) but must be checked before launch; **Missing** = still a visible `[placeholder]`.

## Applied (proposed defaults)

- Contact emails: support@, drivers@, privacy@, hello@ `vukaride.co.za` (in `.env.production`, shown on Contact, Drive FAQ, Delete account, legal pages).
- Support hours on the Contact page; driver support hours 06:00 to 22:00.
- Cash or card payment FAQ; in-app help (shield button, share trip, call support, SAPS 10111 and 112).
- Driver verification steps and the eight-point driver promise (Safety page).
- Weekly Wednesday card payouts; cash-trip fee handling; 2 to 5 working day verification wording.
- Mission: "To make every journey in Africa safe, fair and dignified, for riders and drivers alike."
- Values: Safety, Dignity and Reliability use the proposed lines. **Community uses the earlier softer line**, not "we hire locally and invest", because the proposal itself says to claim that only if you will.
- Delete-account page: in-app path, deleted data, 3-day confirmation and 30-day completion, retention table.
- Privacy and Terms outlines filled with the proposed data lists, retention table, cancellation and refund rules, governing law.
- "Coming soon" store badges and a "Notify me" box on the home page until store URLs are set.
- Launch city dropdown: `[City 1]`, `[City 2]`, "Other (tell us where)" plus an optional "where are you?" field.

## Confirm before launch

- Every feature claim must match the app: shield/safety button, share trip, one-tap call, receipts, card payments, weekly payouts, delete-account path, "Menu, Help, Driver support".
- Driver requirements, PrDP age (21), e-hailing operating licence and vehicle branding: check with the Provincial Regulatory Entity. The licence fee is deliberately **not** published.
- All retention periods (5 years, 3 years, 12 months, 2 years, 24 months) and the "do not sell personal information" line: attorney and accountant.
- Whether Vuka Ride must register with the National Public Transport Regulator and as a local company.
- Panic-button duties in vehicles (not mentioned on the site).
- Domain availability and that the four mailboxes exist.

## Missing (needs you)

Phone, WhatsApp, street address, launch cities, store URLs, social URLs, registered company name and number, VAT number, Information Officer, commission percentage (or decision to keep it private), vehicle age and class, insurance cover, cancellation fee amount, founding story, services list and data hosting region, security measures, expected response time on the contact form, PAIA manual link, notice approach for policy changes.
